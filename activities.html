Activities

Georgia Tech Climbing Club – Treasurer

As Treasurer of the Georgia Tech Climbing Club, I managed the finances for a group of over 150 active members. This included budgeting over $15,000 annually, handling dues and reimbursements, and preparing funding presentations. My role required a balance of organization, communication, and leadership to support events, competitions, and gear purchases while maintaining financial transparency.

Alpha Epsilon Pi – Brother at Large

Within Alpha Epsilon Pi, I served as the Brother at Large — a role focused on maintaining morale, building brotherhood, and advocating for mental health. I organized five mental wellness initiatives throughout the year and worked to make the fraternity a more inclusive and supportive community. This position also gave me experience with conflict resolution and executive leadership.

Competitive Programming Club – Team Member

I was also a member of Georgia Tech's Competitive Programming Club, where I participated in regular algorithmic challenges and programming contests. It was a great environment to strengthen my problem-solving skills and collaborate with peers who are equally passionate about computational thinking.
